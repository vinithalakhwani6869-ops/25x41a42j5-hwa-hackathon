Privacy & your interest

Time plus smell is a proposed concept product. This page explains how your information is handled when you use the contact form.

Information you provide

The contact form asks for your name, email address, and an optional message. This information is only used to respond to your expression of interest in Time plus smell and to follow development updates.

Contact form — not a live message service

The contact form does not send actual messages as of now. It is a front-end demonstration only: when you submit the form, a local confirmation is shown, but no message is transmitted, stored, or delivered to any recipient. No email is sent and no data is collected by us.

Because no message is sent, no personal data is stored, shared, or sold. We do not use cookies or analytics to track you.

Concept notice

Time plus smell is a concept model only. It does not exist as a real product, and no performance, safety, or effectiveness claims are made.
