Our technology

Five decisions, one idea.

Why Time plus smell is built the way it is — and why it differs from a conventional dog toy.

🔱

Why scent-based enrichment?

A dog's nose is its primary way of understanding the world. Scent work engages the brain deeply and tires a dog out in a calm, satisfying way — without repetitive motion.

🔂

Why a rotating drum?

A cylinder with multiple chambers lets us sequence activities automatically. One drum rotation brings a fresh challenge into position, then seals the last one away.

🔒

Why one accessible chamber?

A single shutter means only one chamber is open at a time. The dog focuses on the current puzzle instead of rushing between every reward at once.

🔏

Why mechanical operation?

A hand-wound spring drives a mechanical timing system. No batteries to charge, no electronics to fail, no screens for a dog to ignore.

📌

Why interchangeable modules?

Chambers take swap-in scent modules — sniff, slide, lift, search. New modules keep the experience novel as the dog learns the old ones.

Concept notice: Time plus smell is a proposed model and does not exist as a real product yet. No performance, safety, or effectiveness claims are made. This page describes a concept only.
