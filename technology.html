Our technology

Five decisions, one idea.

Why Time plus smell is built the way it is — and how it differs from a conventional dog toy.

🔱

Why scent-based enrichment?

A dog's nose is its primary way of understanding the world. Scent work engages the brain deeply — calm, satisfying, and mentally tiring in the best way — without relying on repetitive motion that a dog quickly masters.

🔂

Why a rotating drum?

A cylinder with multiple enclosed chambers lets us sequence activities automatically. One rotation brings a fresh challenge into position while the previous chamber seals away, preserving novelty across the day.

🔒

Why one accessible chamber?

A single shutter means only one chamber is open at a time. This prevents the dog from rushing between every reward at once and keeps the dog focused on solving the current scent puzzle.

🔏

Why mechanical operation?

A hand-wound spring drives a mechanical timing system. No batteries to charge, no screens for a dog to ignore, and no electronics that can fail — just reliable, quiet clockwork.

📌

Why interchangeable modules?

Chambers accept swap-in scent modules — sniff, slide, lift, search. As a dog learns one module, a new one can be inserted to keep the experience novel over time.

Concept notice: Time plus smell is a proposed model and does not exist as a real product yet. No performance, safety, or effectiveness claims are made.
